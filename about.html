Who We Are

A Studio With a Point of View

We Build Apps

We build iOS applications that people actually want to use- not because they have to — because the tool is genuinely useful, maybe even a little fun. That's the standard we hold ourselves to.

We Consult

We work with organizations that need a steady hand on digital projects — someone who knows the territory, communicates clearly, and can help teams move with purpose. No fluff, no jargon. We want to help get the right things done.

We're Honest

We'll tell you when we don't know something. We'll tell you when an idea probably won't work, and why. We'd rather have a real conversation than a polished one that leads nowhere useful.

We're Based in Omaha

Transversal Labs is a Nebraska born, built from the ground up in Omaha. We work with clients and collaborators anywhere — but this is home, and we're proud of it.

Our Philosophy

Technology Serves People.
Not the Other Way Around.

We've worked in and around technology long enough to know that the tool is rarely the point. The people using it are what matters most. How well a piece of software helps someone do something — or delights them in the process — that's the goal and the metric. If the tool doesn't help you do something, it's not a good one.

We believe that doing good work doesn't have to feel like a grind. We're seasoned professionals who take quality seriously. We just don't think "serious" and "enjoyable" are mutually exclusive.

01

Plain Language, Always

We value simple, accurate communication. We skip the jargon and say what we mean.

02

Curiosity Over Certainty

We ask questions. We'd rather get to the right answer slowly than rush to a wrong one. And when we don't know something, we say so.

03

People First, Technology Second

Every decision we make starts user. What do they need? What would actually help them? What path, what choice, what decision will postiviely affect their expereince? These are the questions that drive everything we do.

04

Work Can Be Fun

Remember when apps used to be genuinely exciting to discover? We're trying to bring that back — in what we build, and in how we work.
